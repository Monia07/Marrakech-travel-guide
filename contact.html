Travel basics for visiting Marrakech

  • Best time to visit: Spring and autumn (don't forget your sunblock).
  • Travel with cash as paying with a card is not always possible, especially when shopping in markets and taking a taxi.
  • The price is always negotiable so try to bargain before agreeing to pay.
